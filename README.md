I'm Ananya. I work at the intersection of **quantitative finance, machine learning, and optimization**, with an interest in how mathematical models become useful research tools.

My projects explore exercise decisions, market microstructure, and AI-assisted financial research. I care about thoughtful baselines, reproducible experiments, and understanding what the evidence actually supports.
